Ye kaise kaam karta hai

1

2

AI sawaal ko sahi IMC department se match karta hai, ya batata hai ki ye IMC ka mamla hai hi nahi.

3

Jawaab sirf verified knowledge base aur database se banta hai — phone number ya fees kabhi invent nahi kiye jaate.

4

Agar mamla IMC ka nahi (jaise ghar ki bijli), to sahi authority aur unka number seedha bata diya jaata hai.
