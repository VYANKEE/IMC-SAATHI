Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Chaar seedhe steps mein aapki shikayat ya sawaal sahi jagah pahunchta hai.

1

Apna vibhaag (department) chunein, ya seedha apna sawaal likh dein — English, Hindi ya Hinglish, jaisa aap bolte hain.

2

IMC Saathi aapke sawaal ko sahi department se match karta hai — ya batata hai ki ye IMC ka mamla hai hi nahi.

3

Jawaab sirf verified knowledge base aur database se banta hai — phone number, fees ya procedure kabhi invent nahi kiye jaate.

4

Agar mamla IMC ka nahi hai (jaise ghar ki bijli), to sahi authority aur unka number seedha bata diya jaata hai.

Why IMC Saathi

Ek assistant jo sirf wahi batata hai jo verify ho sake.

Verified facts only

Phone number, fees, office timing — sab database se aate hain, AI kabhi khud se nahi banata.

Teen bhashaon mein baat

English, Hindi ya Hinglish — jaisa aap type karein, wahi samjha jaata hai.

Smart routing

Har civic sawaal IMC ka nahi hota — jab zarurat ho, sahi authority (jaise bijli company) seedha bata diya jaata hai.

Saaf refusal, jhoothi jaankari nahi

Agar pakki jaankari nahi hai, to assistant seedha keh deta hai — kabhi andaza nahi lagata.
